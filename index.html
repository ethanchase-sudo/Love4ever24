Our Love Legacy

Real Love. Real Legacy.
One Story at a Time.

We met in August 2002 at Lincoln University, Pennsylvania — but the story started years before, in New York City, two kids growing up a single train stop apart who had somehow never met.

One movie night during freshman year changed everything. Twenty-three years later, we're still laughing, still learning, and still in love. Love4Ever24 was born so other couples could build that same kind of lasting connection — on purpose.

"Our love isn't luck. It's destiny. It's purpose. It's real."
— Kwame & Nyasia Sarfo, "The Sarfos"
